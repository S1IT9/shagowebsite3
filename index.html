What SHAGO does

One app for riders and drivers

The same account switches between booking a ride and driving for income, so a student who rides in the morning can earn in the evening.

Book rides anytime

Choose pickup and drop, see the fare before you confirm, and track the captain on the map.

Drive and earn more

Go online when you are free, accept nearby requests and see earnings after every trip.

Safe and secure

Ride OTP, verified captains, live trip sharing and an SOS button that alerts saved contacts.

24/7 support

Report an issue from inside any trip and reach a person any hour of the day.

Purpose and impact

Built for a community need, not just a trip

Theme: Smart Solutions for Community Needs. SHAGO targets the gap between where buses stop and where people actually need to go.

Problem statement

  • Students and daily commuters struggle with the first and last kilometre between campus, hostels, stations and bus stands.
  • Auto fares are often negotiated, and late-evening options are limited.
  • Many people with a bike and free hours have no simple, trusted way to earn from it.

Project objectives

  • Give riders a clear fare before they book.
  • Let any verified bike owner earn part-time with a visible fee.
  • Build safety into every trip with OTP, trip sharing and SOS.
  • Deliver the full lifecycle: idea, design, build, test, deploy, document.

Access for students

Affordable rides at odd hours, from campus gates to stations, hospitals and markets.

Income for drivers

Flexible earning for students, homemakers and gig workers who own a two-wheeler.

Safety by design

Verified captains, ride OTP, live location sharing and an emergency button on every trip.

Lighter on the road

A bike uses less road space and fuel per rider than a car, which helps crowded city streets.
